# LedgerGuard final report

LedgerGuard is a four-week intern billing platform: authenticate a company, isolate its database, write a balanced ledger once, then chart that history.

Week 1 delivered RS256 login and tenant connections.  
Week 2 delivered idempotent atomic billing.  
Week 3 delivered aggregation analytics and the Chart.js dashboard.  
Week 4 delivered request tracing, receipt signatures, hardening, Docker, and CI.

The product is one monorepo (`backend/`, `frontend/`, `shared/`, `docs/`), not four disconnected demos.
